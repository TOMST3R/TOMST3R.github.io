Privacy Policy

rclone-personal is a private application used solely by its owner to access and synchronize the owner's Google Drive files through rclone.

Google user data

The application accesses Google Drive data only as required to read, create, modify, synchronize and delete files requested by the owner.

Data storage

OAuth credentials and authorization tokens are stored locally by rclone on the owner's devices. This website does not collect or store Google Drive data.

Data sharing

Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Purpose

This application is for personal use only and is not made available to other users.
